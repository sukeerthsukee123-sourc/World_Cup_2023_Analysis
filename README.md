# 💳 Credit Card Financial Operations & Risk Analytics Pipeline

An end-to-end data analytics and business intelligence project engineered to analyze credit card transactions, revenue structures, and customer profiles. Utilizing Tableau's high-performance `.hyper` data engine, this project transforms granular transaction ledgers into structured, executive-level visual insights that drive strategic credit risk mitigation and customer engagement models.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

*   **Revenue Architecture Mapping:** Quantify core portfolio income streams, separating revolving interest metrics from fee collections (Swipe, Annual, and Late Fees).
*   **Demographic Behavioral Profiling:** Track wallet share trends and volume distribution across consumer education tiers, income brackets, and job types.
*   **Credit Portfolio Risk Triage:** Isolate default signals by evaluating account delinquency velocity alongside average utilization limits.

---

## 🛠️ Data Infrastructure & Tools
*   **Visualization Suite:** `Tableau Desktop` (Workbook: `Dashboard - Credit Card Analytics.twb`)
*   **Data Storage Layer:** `Tableau Hyper Engine` (High-performance relational analytical data extract)
*   **Core Metrics Tracked:** Gross Transaction Volume (GTV), Credit Utilization Ratio (CUR), Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC), and Delinquency Trends.

---

## 📊 Business Intelligence Metrics

The workbook connects seamlessly to an optimized extract pipeline to deliver deep analytical visibility across two primary operational domains:

### ⚡ 1. Customer Spend Optimization
*   **Category Performance:** Real-time visibility into transaction spikes across merchants (Bills, Grocery, Entertainment, Fuel).
*   **Card Tier Benchmarking:** Compares swipe volumes, average balances, and drop-off rates between Blue, Silver, Gold, and Platinum holders.
*   **Demographic Trends:** Drills down into spend frequency by marital status, education level, and gender cohorts.

### 📉 2. Risk Mitigation & Portfolios
*   **Delinquency Aging Matrix:** Groups active portfolio accounts by time past due (30/60/90+ days DPD) to predict write-offs early.
*   **Credit Exposure Evaluation:** Monitors utilization percentages to identify high-risk cardholders exceeding safe borrowing caps.

---

## 🚀 Setting Up the Project

### Prerequisites
*   Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public (Version 2021.1 or higher recommended).

### Running Locally
1.  Clone this repository to your local architecture.
2.  Ensure the Tableau data extracts (`.hyper` files) are placed correctly within your database or directory.
3.  Open the file `Dashboard - Credit Card Analytics.twb`.
4.  If prompted by Tableau to fix broken links, navigate to and select the localized path of the provided `.hyper` files.
